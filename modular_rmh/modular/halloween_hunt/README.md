# Halloween Candy Hunt

An admin-only event. The storyteller never starts it by itself (`max_occurrences = 0`).

## How to start it

1. Use the admin verb **Trigger Event** (GameMaster tab). You need the FUN right.
2. Find **Halloween: Candy Hunt** in the Mundane tab or the All tab. It is at the bottom of the list. If the list is empty, click **Show Roundstart Events** to switch that view off.
3. Click **Fire**. Set the duration, the candy on the ground, the number of ghosts, and the candy zone.
4. While the hunt runs, the same row shows **Scores** and **End now**.

## What players get

- Every living player gets a pumpkin basket. Players who join late get one 3 seconds after they spawn.
- Candy lies on open floor in the town streets, the farm and public buildings. It does not appear in homes, the chapel, cells, the garrison, the bank or on roofs. With "Town + Outskirts", 30% of the candy lies on outdoor tiles up to 30 tiles from the town.
- The candy refills to the target amount every 2 minutes. 1 candy in 25 is a golden toffee, which is worth 5.
- The basket stores only a number, so the amount of candy has no limit and costs no performance.

## Basket controls

- Put candy into the basket by hand, or click candy or a tile with the basket to scoop up to 25 pieces.
- Use the basket in hand to take one candy out. The candy then no longer counts. If a player eats it, they get a sugar rush (faster movement for 10 seconds) and a good mood.
- Use your basket on another person (not in combat mode) to take 3 candy from their basket. This takes 2.5 seconds, and the other person can walk away to stop it.
- Use one basket on another to pour 5 candy at a time. If one of the two baskets is yours, the candy always goes into yours.
- Only the owner's own basket counts for that owner.

## Ghosts

- Common ghosts use `ghost1` or `ghost2`. One rare hooded haunt (`ghostwithcape`) is faster and scatters more candy.
- Ghosts float through walls and hunt the richest candy carrier near them, including a basket hidden in a bag. Players can outrun them, go to the chapel (ghosts cannot enter consecrated ground), or throw a candy, which a ghost stops to eat.
- A catch freezes the player for 2 seconds and throws out 30% of their candy (40% for the haunt), at least 3 and at most 20. If the player's **Lust Magic Targetable** ERP preference is on, they also climax. After a catch, the ghosts leave that player alone for 20 seconds and the ghost reappears elsewhere 15 seconds later.
- If nobody carries candy on a ghost's floor for 30 seconds, the ghost moves to a floor where somebody does.

## End and prizes

- The fullest basket glows during the hunt. Standings are announced every 5 minutes, and a warning comes 1 minute before the end.
- At the end, the ghosts vanish and the candy on the ground is removed. Players keep their baskets.
- 1st place (all players tied for 1st) gets the crown of the Pumpkin Monarch and the event-only title **Pumpkin Monarch**. The crown does not hide the face, so the wearer stays recognisable. The title is switched on automatically and shows after the player's name, also in later rounds. Players can hide it at the abyssal delve gate in the Delver's Ledger tab.
- The next two players each get a lit pumpkin lamptern and 5 gold coins.
